Towards small-scale artificial collectives

How can we build small robotic swarms that sense, decide, and act collectively with minimal resources?

We develop small-scale robot swarms that autonomously explore challenging environments — designing robots that perceive and act with limited resources, coordinate for collective behaviors, and probe fundamental questions in actuation, power, fluid mechanics, and collective intelligence. Our work sits where microsystems meet swarm robotics, and it is inspired by nature: fish schools that move, forage, and save energy together.
